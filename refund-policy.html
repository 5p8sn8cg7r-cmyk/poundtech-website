# PoundTech Refund & Returns Policy **Last Updated:** 28 September 2026 PoundTech is an online automotive marketplace that connects customers with automotive suppliers and service providers. This Refund & Returns Policy explains how refunds, returns and cancellations are handled when purchasing products or services through PoundTech. ## 1. Product Purchases Products purchased through PoundTech may be subject to the individual supplier's applicable return and warranty conditions, provided those conditions comply with applicable South African consumer law. Customers should inspect products upon delivery or collection and contact PoundTech as soon as reasonably possible if a product: * Arrives damaged; * Is defective; * Is materially different from the product description; or * Is the wrong product. Where appropriate, PoundTech may request photographs, order information or other evidence to assist with resolving the matter. ## 2. Change of Mind Where a customer changes their mind about a purchase, the availability of a return or refund may depend on the applicable supplier's policy and the nature of the product. Certain products may not be eligible for return where they have been used, installed, modified, damaged after delivery, or otherwise fall outside applicable return requirements. Any applicable rights provided by South African consumer protection law will not be excluded by this policy. ## 3. Defective or Incorrect Products If a product is defective or incorrect, customers should contact PoundTech with: * Order number; * Customer name; * Product name; * Description of the issue; and * Supporting photographs or other relevant information where available. PoundTech will review the matter and may liaise with the relevant supplier to determine the appropriate resolution. Depending on the circumstances, the resolution may include repair, replacement, credit or refund, subject to applicable law. ## 4. Mechanic Services and Bookings Mechanic services arranged through PoundTech are provided by independent mechanics. If a customer needs to cancel a booking, they should contact the relevant mechanic as soon as possible. Refunds for deposits or payments relating to mechanic services will depend on the circumstances of the cancellation, the mechanic's applicable terms, and South African consumer law. PoundTech may assist customers and mechanics in resolving disputes but does not provide the underlying mechanical service itself. ## 5. Refund Processing Where a refund is approved, the refund will generally be processed using the original payment method where reasonably possible. Processing times may vary depending on the payment provider and the customer's bank or financial institution. PoundTech will communicate the applicable refund process to the customer once the refund has been approved. ## 6. Delivery and Return Costs Where a product must be returned because it is defective, incorrect or otherwise covered by applicable consumer rights, the responsibility for reasonable return or collection costs will be determined in accordance with applicable law and the circumstances of the transaction. For voluntary returns or change-of-mind requests, any applicable return or delivery costs may depend on the supplier's return policy and applicable law. ## 7. How to Request a Refund or Return Customers should contact PoundTech with their order details and a description of the issue. Please include your: * Full name; * Order number; * Contact details; * Product or service concerned; and * Reason for requesting the refund or return. PoundTech will review the request and provide further instructions where necessary. ## 8. Marketplace Responsibility PoundTech operates as an online marketplace connecting customers with independent suppliers and service providers. Where a transaction is fulfilled by a supplier or mechanic, PoundTech may facilitate communication and payment processing while the supplier or service provider remains responsible for fulfilling the underlying product or service obligations. Nothing in this policy is intended to limit or remove any rights a customer may have under applicable South African law. ## 9.
