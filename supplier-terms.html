By applying to become a PoundTech supplier or listing products on the platform, the supplier agrees to these terms. ## 1. PoundTech Marketplace PoundTech operates an online automotive marketplace that connects customers with automotive product suppliers. PoundTech provides the platform through which suppliers may list products, receive orders and communicate with customers where applicable. Unless expressly agreed otherwise, PoundTech does not purchase products from suppliers for resale. Suppliers remain responsible for fulfilling customer orders. ## 2. Supplier Eligibility To become a supplier, a business must provide accurate information during the application process. PoundTech may request additional information or documentation to verify a supplier before approving its application. PoundTech may reject or suspend an application where information provided is incomplete, inaccurate or cannot reasonably be verified. ## 3. Supplier Information Suppliers must ensure that the following information remains accurate: * Business name; * Contact person's name; * Telephone number; * Email address; * Business address; * Service or operating area; * Business type; and * Other information reasonably required by PoundTech. Suppliers must notify PoundTech of material changes to their business information. ## 4. Product Listings Suppliers are responsible for ensuring that product listings are accurate and up to date. Product listings should include accurate information such as: * Product name; * Description; * Category; * Price; * Stock availability; * Vehicle compatibility where applicable; * Images; and * Relevant specifications. Suppliers must not knowingly provide misleading or fraudulent product information. ## 5. Pricing Suppliers are responsible for maintaining accurate product prices. Suppliers must not intentionally advertise one price and attempt to charge a customer a different price after an order has been placed, except where permitted by applicable law or where an obvious pricing error has occurred. PoundTech may take reasonable action where pricing information appears misleading or materially inaccurate. ## 6. Stock Availability Suppliers are responsible for maintaining accurate stock information. If a supplier is unable to fulfil an accepted order because a product is unavailable, the supplier must notify PoundTech as soon as reasonably possible. Repeated failure to maintain accurate stock information may result in suspension or removal from the marketplace. ## 7. Order Fulfilment Suppliers are responsible for fulfilling accepted orders accurately and within the applicable delivery or collection timeframe communicated to the customer. Suppliers must provide the correct product in the correct quantity and in an appropriate condition. Where delivery is handled by the supplier, the supplier is responsible for the applicable delivery arrangements. ## 8. Returns, Refunds and Defective Products Suppliers must comply with applicable South African consumer-protection laws. Suppliers may be required to assist PoundTech in resolving customer complaints involving products supplied by them. Where a product is defective, incorrect or otherwise subject to a lawful return or refund, the supplier must cooperate with the applicable resolution process. PoundTech's customer-facing Refund & Returns Policy provides additional information regarding customer refund and return procedures. ## 9. Product Compliance Suppliers are responsible for ensuring that products listed on PoundTech comply with applicable laws, regulations, safety requirements and industry requirements. Suppliers must not list counterfeit, stolen, prohibited or unlawfully imported products. Where applicable, suppliers should maintain documentation demonstrating the authenticity, legality or compliance of products offered through the marketplace. ## 10. Customer Information Suppliers may receive customer information where reasonably necessary to fulfil an order. Supplier access to customer information must be limited to legitimate business purposes connected with the PoundTech transaction. Suppliers must not sell, distribute or misuse customer information. Suppliers must handle personal information in accordance with applicable privacy and data-protection laws. ## 11. Marketplace Fees PoundTech may charge suppliers fees for participating in the marketplace. Applicable fees may include commissions, transaction fees, listing fees, subscription fees or other charges communicated to the supplier. Where fees apply, PoundTech will communicate the applicable fee structure before or when the relevant service is activated. PoundTech may update its fee structure from time to time subject to reasonable notice and any applicable agreement. ## 12. Payments Customer payments may be processed through third-party payment providers integrated with PoundTech. Where applicable, PoundTech may receive payment on behalf of or in connection with marketplace transactions and subsequently account for the supplier's applicable amount after deducting authorised fees, refunds, chargebacks or other applicable adjustments. The exact settlement process may depend on the payment arrangement approved for the PoundTech marketplace. ## 13.
