Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

PoundTech respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with and what rights you have, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

1. Who We Are

PoundTech is an online automotive marketplace in South Africa. For the purposes of POPIA, PoundTech is the responsible party for the personal information described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account information: your email address and login details when you create an account.
  • Order information: your name, phone number, email address, delivery address and the products you order.
  • Vehicle information: the vehicles you save in My Garage, such as make, model, year, engine and nickname, used to show you compatible products.
  • Quotation and booking information: details you share when requesting quotations or bookings from mechanics.
  • Supplier and mechanic information: business details and supporting documents provided when applying to sell or offer services.
  • Communications: messages you send to us.
  • Technical information: basic data such as browser type and device information, collected through the services that run the site.

3. Why We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your orders and handle returns and refunds;
  • To show products that fit your saved vehicles;
  • To connect you with suppliers and mechanics;
  • To provide customer support and communicate about your orders;
  • To detect fraud and keep the platform secure;
  • To keep records required by law; and
  • To improve the website and our services.

4. Who We Share It With

We only share personal information where it is needed to provide our service:

  • Suppliers receive the order details they need to fulfil and deliver your order.
  • Mechanics receive the information you submit in quotation requests and bookings.
  • Payment providers process payments. We do not store your full card details.
  • Service providers that host our website, run our database and authentication, and provide our email systems.
  • Authorities where we are required to do so by law.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Where Information Is Stored

Our service providers may store or process information on servers outside South Africa. Where this happens, we take reasonable steps to make sure the information is protected to a standard comparable to POPIA.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

PoundTech stores your shopping cart in your browser's local storage so that it is still there when you return. Our authentication service also keeps a login session in your browser while you are signed in. You can clear this at any time through your browser settings, but your cart and login will be reset.

7. How Long We Keep Information

We keep personal information only for as long as it is needed for the purposes above, or as required by law. Order records are kept for the period required for tax and accounting purposes. Vehicles saved in My Garage can be removed by you at any time.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect personal information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. No online system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a security compromise affecting your information occurs, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you;
  • Ask us to correct or delete information that is inaccurate, outdated or no longer needed;
  • Object to the processing of your information in certain circumstances;
  • Withdraw your consent where we rely on it; and
  • Complain to the Information Regulator if you believe your information has been handled unlawfully.

To exercise these rights, email info@poundtech.co.za. We may need to verify your identity first.

The Information Regulator can be contacted through its website at inforegulator.org.za.

10. Children

PoundTech is not intended for people under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page with the date it was last updated.
