# PoundTech Privacy Policy **Last Updated:** 28 September 2026 PoundTech respects the privacy of users of its website and marketplace. This Privacy Policy explains how PoundTech may collect, use, store and protect personal information. By using the PoundTech website, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. ## 1. Information We May Collect Depending on how you use PoundTech, we may collect information including: ### Customer information * Name; * Email address; * Telephone number; * Vehicle information; * Delivery or collection information; * Order information; * Mechanic quotation and booking information; and * Communications submitted through the platform. ### Supplier information Suppliers may provide: * Business name; * Contact person's name; * Telephone number; * Email address; * Business address; * Business type; * Product information; and * Other information required to verify and operate a supplier account. ### Mechanic information Mechanics may provide: * Business name; * Contact person's name; * Telephone number; * Email address; * Business address; * Service area; * Services offered; * Business description; and * Other information required to operate a mechanic account. ## 2. How We Collect Information Information may be collected when you: * Create or use an account; * Submit a supplier or mechanic application; * Place an order; * Request a quotation; * Accept a quotation; * Make a booking; * Submit a review; * Contact PoundTech; or * Interact with features of the website. Technical information may also be collected automatically through hosting, security, analytics or other third-party services used by the platform. ## 3. How We Use Information PoundTech may use personal information to: * Process and manage orders; * Facilitate payments; * Connect customers with suppliers and mechanics; * Process quotation requests; * Manage mechanic bookings; * Communicate with users; * Provide customer support; * Verify suppliers and mechanics; * Prevent fraud and misuse; * Maintain and improve the platform; * Maintain records required for business and legal purposes; and * Comply with applicable law. ## 4. Sharing Information PoundTech may share relevant information with marketplace participants where reasonably necessary to provide the requested service. For example, information submitted in a mechanic quotation request may be shared with the mechanic selected by the customer so that the mechanic can respond to the request. Order information may be shared with the relevant supplier to enable fulfilment. PoundTech may also share information with third-party service providers where necessary to operate the platform, including payment providers, hosting providers, authentication providers and other technology providers. PoundTech does not sell personal information to third parties for their independent marketing purposes. ## 5. Payments Payments may be processed by third-party payment service providers. When a customer makes a payment, certain information may be provided to the payment provider to process the transaction. PoundTech does not intend to store full payment-card details on its own website systems unless expressly stated otherwise. Payment providers may have their own privacy policies and terms governing their processing of personal information. ## 6. Information Security PoundTech takes reasonable measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure. However, no internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should protect their account credentials and avoid sharing passwords with others. ## 7. Data Retention PoundTech may retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including to provide services, maintain business records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud and comply with legal obligations. When information is no longer reasonably required, it may be deleted, anonymised or securely disposed of, subject to applicable legal requirements. ## 8. These technologies may be used for purposes such as: * Remembering shopping-cart information; * Maintaining user preferences; * Supporting authentication; * Improving website functionality; and * Understanding how the website is used. Users may be able to control cookies through their browser settings, although disabling certain technologies may affect website functionality. ## 9.
