Before you hit the road.

A quick reminder for every driver travelling this December. Take a few minutes to prepare before setting off.

Send this checklist to someone you care about.
Check tyres and spare wheel
Test brakes and lights
Check oil and coolant
Inspect wipers and washer fluid
Pack a warning triangle
Carry a first-aid kit
Bring a torch and basic tools
Plan rest stops and breaks
Buckle up every passenger
Never drive tired or impaired

Remember

Follow the rules of the road, obey speed limits, maintain a safe following distance and never use your phone while driving. Check current road and weather conditions before departure. This checklist is general guidance and does not replace a professional vehicle inspection.
