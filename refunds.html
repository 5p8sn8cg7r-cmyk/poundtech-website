Refund & Returns Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This policy explains how returns, cancellations and refunds work for products bought through PoundTech. Products are supplied by independent suppliers, and PoundTech helps coordinate returns and refunds on their behalf.

Nothing in this policy limits your rights under the Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 (CPA), the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act 25 of 2002 (ECTA) or any other South African law.

1. Your Right to Cancel

If you buy products online, you may cancel the transaction without giving a reason and without penalty within 7 days of receiving the goods, as provided for in the ECTA.

To qualify, products must be:

  • Unused and uninstalled;
  • In their original condition and packaging; and
  • Returned with proof of purchase or your order number.

The direct cost of returning the goods for a change-of-mind return is for the customer's account, unless the return is for one of the reasons in section 2.

2. Faulty, Damaged or Incorrect Products

You are entitled to a repair, replacement or refund, at no cost to you, where a product:

  • Arrives damaged;
  • Is defective or does not work as it should;
  • Is not what you ordered, or not as described; or
  • Is unsafe or not of a reasonable quality.

Under the CPA, you may return defective goods within 6 months of delivery. Please report problems as soon as you notice them, and keep the packaging where possible.

3. Fitment

Many automotive parts are vehicle-specific. PoundTech shows fitment information where the supplier provides it, but you are responsible for checking that a product is compatible with your vehicle before you buy, especially for products marked “Universal — check fitment” or “Fitment not specified”.

If a product marked as compatible with your selected vehicle does not fit, contact us and we will arrange a return or exchange.

4. Items That Cannot Be Returned

Unless faulty, damaged or incorrect, the following cannot be returned:

  • Products that have been installed, used or modified;
  • Products made, ordered or customised specifically for you;
  • Opened consumables such as fluids, oils and chemicals; and
  • Products missing parts, packaging or labels that were supplied with them.

5. How to Return a Product

  1. Email orders@poundtech.co.za within the time limits above.
  2. Include your order number (it starts with “PT”), your name, the product and the reason for the return. Photos help if the product is damaged or incorrect.
  3. We will respond within 2 business days with the return instructions, including where to send the product.
  4. Pack the product securely. Please do not send items back before you have heard from us.

6. Refunds

  • Approved refunds are paid back to the original payment method, or by EFT where that is not possible.
  • Refunds are processed within 7 business days of the returned goods being received and inspected, and in any event within 30 days of the cancellation or return being approved.
  • Your bank may need extra time to show the refund on your account.

7. Cancelling an Order Before It Ships

If your order has not yet been dispatched by the supplier, contact orders@poundtech.co.za as soon as possible and we will try to cancel it. If it has already shipped, the return process above applies.

8. Orders We Cannot Fulfil

If a supplier cannot fulfil your order, for example because of a stock or pricing error, we will let you know and refund any payment in full.

9. Mechanic Services

Services booked with independent mechanics are not covered by this policy. Cancellations, deposits and refunds for services are subject to the mechanic's terms and applicable South African law, as set out in our Terms & Conditions. If you have a problem, contact us and we will help where we can.
