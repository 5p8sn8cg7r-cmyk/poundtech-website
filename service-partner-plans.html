Plan questions

Can I start for free?

Yes. Free Partner is designed to let your business get listed and begin receiving customer enquiries and bookings without a monthly membership fee.

Is the monthly fee the same as booking commission?

No. A paid membership fee is separate from commission on eligible completed bookings.

When can I upgrade?

Paid memberships will become available once recurring billing is enabled and the advertised benefits are ready. Your existing bookings will keep the commission terms recorded when they were created.

How is Enterprise priced?

Enterprise membership starts from R999 per month. Commission and the final package are agreed according to the size and requirements of the operation.
