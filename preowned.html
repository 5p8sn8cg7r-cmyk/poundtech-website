Find pre-owned car parts in South Africa

PoundTech’s pre-owned marketplace is a place to discover used and refurbished automotive parts offered by private sellers. Buying pre-owned can be a useful option when you are looking for a particular component or comparing more affordable alternatives to new parts.

What should you check before buying?

  • Vehicle compatibility: Confirm the make, model, year, engine and, where relevant, the exact part number with the seller before agreeing to buy.
  • Condition: Read the stated condition carefully and ask the seller about wear, damage, repairs or missing components.
  • Photos and details: Review the listing images and description. Ask for additional information if anything is unclear.
  • Collection or delivery: Confirm the available handover arrangements and any related costs directly before completing a transaction.

Buying from private sellers

Pre-owned parts are listed by private sellers. Listings are sold as-is and are not inspected or guaranteed by PoundTech. Buyers should make their own checks, ask questions and confirm that a part is suitable for their vehicle before making a purchase.
