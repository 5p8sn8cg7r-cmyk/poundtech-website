How to process an order

Open the Orders tab, select an order to see its details, then use the Status dropdown to record each stage.

Pending→Accepted→Processing→Shipped→Completed
1
Pending — Accept the orderA customer has paid. Check the items and quantities, confirm you can fulfil the order, then change the status to Accepted.
2
Accepted — You have confirmed itThe order is yours to fulfil. Move it to Processing when you start preparing or packing it.
3
Processing — Prepare the orderPick the correct products, check the quantities and get the parcel ready for collection or delivery.
4
Shipped — It has left your handsOnly mark an order as Shipped once it has been handed to the courier or dispatched to the customer.
5
Completed — The order has been fulfilledMark it Completed once the customer has received the order or the agreed fulfilment is finished. Completion makes the order eligible for the payout process; any new-supplier payout hold still applies.
Important: Do not mark an order Completed just because you have packed it or handed it to a courier. Keep the status accurate so customers and PoundTech can follow the order correctly.

Quick tips

  • Check the New orders count whenever you sign in.
  • Work through older outstanding orders first.
  • Keep product stock accurate so you do not sell items you cannot supply.
  • Check Payments & Earnings for payout timing and any new-supplier hold information.
