Delivery Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This policy explains how products ordered through PoundTech are delivered. Products are supplied and dispatched by independent suppliers, so delivery times can vary between products and orders.

1. Where We Deliver

We deliver to addresses within South Africa. Delivery to some remote areas may take longer or may not be available. If we cannot deliver to your address, we will contact you and refund any payment.

2. Processing Time

After you place an order, the supplier confirms it and prepares it for dispatch. This normally takes 1 to 3 business days. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays are processed on the next business day.

3. Delivery Times

Once dispatched, delivery usually takes 2 to 7 business days, depending on your location and the supplier's courier. These are estimates, not guarantees. Delays can be caused by courier issues, public holidays, weather and stock problems.

4. Delivery Costs

Where a delivery fee applies, it will be shown before you complete your order. Fees may vary by supplier, product size and location.

5. Orders From More Than One Supplier

If your order contains products from different suppliers, they may be dispatched separately and arrive on different days.

6. Tracking Your Order

You can check the status of your order on our Track Order page using the order number you received when you placed your order. Where the courier provides a tracking number, we will pass it on.

7. Delivery Address

Please make sure the delivery address and contact number you give us are correct and complete. If a delivery fails because of an incorrect address, or because no one is available to receive it, extra delivery costs may apply.

8. Receiving Your Order

Please check the parcel on delivery. If it is visibly damaged, note this with the courier if possible, and contact us within 48 hours at orders@poundtech.co.za with your order number and photos. Ownership and risk in the products pass to you on delivery.

9. Late, Lost or Damaged Orders

If your order is late, lost or damaged, contact us and we will follow up with the supplier and courier. If an order cannot be delivered, we will arrange a replacement or refund in line with our Refund & Returns Policy.
