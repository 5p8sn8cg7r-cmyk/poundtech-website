Delivery Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy explains how products ordered through PoundTech are delivered. Products are supplied and dispatched by independent suppliers, so delivery times can vary between products and orders.

1. Where We Deliver

We deliver to addresses within South Africa. Delivery to some remote areas may take longer or may not be available. If we cannot deliver to your address, we will contact you and refund any payment.

2. Supplier Dispatch Time

Each supplier sets a typical dispatch timeframe, such as same business day, within 48 hours, 3–5 business days or 7–14 business days. The timeframe shown on a product page is an estimate of when the supplier expects to hand the order to a courier; it is not a guaranteed arrival date. Orders placed after a supplier's cutoff time, on weekends or on public holidays may be processed on the next business day.

3. Estimated Delivery Dates

Product pages, the cart and checkout display an estimated arrival date range. For example, an order placed on the 1st of a month will show an estimated delivery window of the 7th to the 14th. The dates are indicative and may vary by supplier, courier, destination and product availability. Orders containing products from different suppliers may arrive separately.

4. Delivery Costs and PoundTech Delivery Offer

Delivery fees are shown before you complete your order and may vary by supplier, product size and location. Eligible products sold by PoundTech Automotive Store qualify for a delivery discount when the PoundTech Automotive Store portion of the order reaches R1,000. The discount is capped at R200 per order; any delivery cost above that amount remains payable by the customer. Heavy or oversized items weighing 16 kg or more are excluded. This offer does not automatically apply to products sold by independent marketplace suppliers, whose delivery fees are set separately.

5. Orders From More Than One Supplier

If your order contains products from different suppliers, they may be dispatched separately and arrive on different days.

6. Tracking Your Order

You can check the status of your order on our Track Order page using the order number you received when you placed your order. Where the courier provides a tracking number, we will pass it on.

7. Delivery Address

Please make sure the delivery address and contact number you give us are correct and complete. If a delivery fails because of an incorrect address, or because no one is available to receive it, extra delivery costs may apply.

8. Receiving Your Order

Please check the parcel on delivery. If it is visibly damaged, note this with the courier if possible, and contact us within 48 hours at orders@poundtech.co.za with your order number and photos. Ownership and risk in the products pass to you on delivery.

9. Late, Lost or Damaged Orders

If your order is late, lost or damaged, contact us and we will follow up with the supplier and courier. If an order cannot be delivered, we will arrange a replacement or refund in line with our Refund & Returns Policy.
