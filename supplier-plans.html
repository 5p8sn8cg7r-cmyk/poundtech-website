Plan questions

When does my 30-day launch offer begin?

Your introductory period is individual to your supplier account and starts from your registration date. It does not depend on when another supplier joins or on a shared launch date.

Can I start on Standard and upgrade later?

Yes. You can begin on the free Standard plan and enquire about a paid plan as your business grows.

Can I manage multiple staff or branches?

Pro, Business and Enterprise are designed for larger teams. Business and Enterprise account structures will be confirmed as those plans are rolled out.

How does Enterprise pricing work?

Enterprise pricing and commission are tailored to the supplier's size, sales volume, locations and integration requirements.
