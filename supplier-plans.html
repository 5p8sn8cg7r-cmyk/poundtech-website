Plan questions

Can I start on Standard and upgrade later?

Yes. You can begin with the free Standard plan and move to Pro when your business needs the additional tools and lower commission.

Does Pro change how customers shop?

No. Your products remain available through the same PoundTech marketplace. Pro adds supplier-side tools and benefits.

How many people can use a Pro supplier account?

Pro supports up to three named users in total: the business owner and two team members.
